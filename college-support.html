College Planning & Access

More Than an Application.

The path to college begins long before an application is submitted. We help students explore their options, understand what it takes to get there, and build a personalized path toward their educational goals.

01 — Explore

College Exploration

Discover colleges, majors, programs, and educational pathways that align with each student’s interests, strengths, and future goals.

02 — Prepare

College Readiness & Planning

Create a clear path forward through academic planning, application timelines, college requirements, and personalized next steps.

03 — Apply

Applications & Essay Support

Navigate the application process with support for strategy, brainstorming, reviewing, and editing essays while helping students communicate their experiences authentically.

04 — Access

Financial Aid & Scholarships

Help students and families understand FAFSA, financial aid, scholarships, and other resources that can make college more accessible and affordable.
