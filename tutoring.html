Common Questions

One-on-One FAQ

How do I know which program is right for my student?

Every student begins with The Purpose Plan, which helps us recommend the appropriate program and tutoring frequency based on their needs and goals.

How often will my student meet with a tutor?

Frequency is personalized. Students may meet weekly or multiple times per week depending on their goals and recommended level of support.

Are payment options available?

Yes. Payment options are available for select programs and are discussed before enrollment.

Is pricing the same for online and in-person tutoring?

Yes. Standard program rates apply to both. Travel fees may apply for select in-person locations, and rates may vary for advanced or specialized subjects.

How long do I have to complete my tutoring program?

Program timelines are personalized based on your student’s Purpose Plan, recommended tutoring frequency, and academic goals. Some students may complete their hours more quickly than others. Your anticipated program timeline will be established at enrollment, and sessions are intended to be completed according to the agreed-upon schedule.
