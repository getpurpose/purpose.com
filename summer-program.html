Common Questions

Summer On Purpose Frequently Asked Questions

When will summer courses and schedules be released?

Summer course offerings, grade levels, session dates, and schedules will be released ahead of the summer season. Families who join our interest list will be among the first to receive program and enrollment details.

How do I know which summer track is right for my student?

Summer learning is personalized around each student’s goals. Whether your student is preparing for the next grade, ready for advanced learning, or interested in exploring something new, we’ll help families identify the experience that best fits their needs.

What ages and grade levels will summer programs serve?

Summer offerings will be available across multiple grade levels, with specific age and grade requirements listed when each course and session is released.

Will all summer programs be offered every session?

Course offerings may vary throughout the summer based on age group, session, enrollment, and availability. Specific offerings will be announced before enrollment opens.
