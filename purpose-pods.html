Common Questions

Purpose Pod FAQ

How are students matched for a Purpose Pod?

Students are thoughtfully matched based on grade level, subject, learning goals, and scheduling compatibility. The Purpose Plan helps us understand each student before determining the right Pod placement.

What if a Purpose Pod isn’t currently available for my student?

Purpose Pods begin once an appropriate student match is available. Families are encouraged to bring their own Pod by enrolling with an eligible sibling, friend, or classmate with similar academic needs and goals. If you don’t already have a Pod partner, your student can join our matching list or begin with One-on-One Tutoring while we work to find the right fit.

What happens if a student leaves the Pod?

Your rate will remain the same for the duration of your current 8-session block. Group size and pricing may be reassessed before the next block.

Why is there an 8-session commitment?

Consistency helps students build relationships, establish a learning rhythm, and make meaningful academic progress together. The 8-session commitment also helps maintain a stable and consistent learning environment for every student in the Pod.
