Common Questions

About The Purpose Plan

Does every student begin with a Purpose Plan?

Yes. Every student begins with The Purpose Plan so we can understand their current strengths, needs, and goals before recommending the right level of support.

What does The Purpose Plan include?

A personalized review of your student’s current needs, goals, priority areas, and recommended path for academic support.

What happens after The Purpose Plan is created?

We recommend the program and level of support that best aligns with your student’s needs and goals.

How is progress reviewed?

Progress is monitored throughout the student’s program, with regular updates to families and adjustments to the plan as needed.
